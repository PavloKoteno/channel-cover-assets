Channel publishing tools

Private desktop publishing tools operated by Pavlo Kotenko for his Nine Tenths (Ko.Discovery) and Polavimo channels. These tools are not offered as a public service.

Ko Discovery Uploader

Uploads owner-selected videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's Nine Tenths / Ko.Discovery YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Polavimo Publisher

Uploads owner-selected videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's Polavimo YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. A separate Instagram integration prepares and publishes owner-selected Reels and covers for @polavimo.

Google OAuth is used to obtain the channel owner's authorization. Uploads are performed through local tools; this website does not offer sign-in or collect credentials.

Support: creativecutspro@gmail.com for Nine Tenths; polavimofilms@gmail.com for Polavimo.
