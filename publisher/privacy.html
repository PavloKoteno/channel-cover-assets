Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026.

This policy covers Ko Discovery Uploader and Polavimo Publisher, private publishing tools operated by Pavlo Kotenko for his own channels.

Data and purpose

The tools use Google OAuth authorization, access and refresh tokens, channel identifiers and channel names to identify the authorized channel and send owner-selected videos, metadata and thumbnails to YouTube. The Instagram integration uses Meta authorization tokens, Page and Instagram identifiers and owner-selected video, caption and cover data to prepare and publish Instagram content.

Storage and sharing

Credentials are stored in local credential files. Encrypted backups may be retained in the owner's private access archive and server backup. Publishing receipts and channel identifiers may be retained in the private project. Credentials are not included in this public website or the public cover repository.

Selected content and the information required to publish it are sent to Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram. Covers intentionally placed on GitHub Pages are publicly accessible and hosted by GitHub. This website has no application sign-in, analytics scripts or credential collection forms; GitHub may process ordinary hosting access logs under its own policies.

Google user data is used only to operate these publishing tools, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or general-purpose AI model training. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Authorization files are retained while the owner uses the integration. Access can be revoked through Google Account permissions or Meta's connected-app settings. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically delete existing YouTube/Instagram posts, local files or encrypted backups. The owner can remove local credentials, receipts and backup copies separately. Uploaded content is managed through the relevant platform.

For access, removal or privacy questions: creativecutspro@gmail.com (Nine Tenths) or polavimofilms@gmail.com (Polavimo).
